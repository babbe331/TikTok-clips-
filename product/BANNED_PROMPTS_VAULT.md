# 🔒 THE BANNED PROMPTS VAULT

### 40 high-leverage AI prompts the algorithm doesn't want you sharing.

You watched the clips. Here's the whole arsenal — copy, paste, and run each one
into any capable AI assistant (Claude, ChatGPT, Gemini). Every prompt is written
to produce a *usable output*, not a vague essay. Swap the **[bracketed]** parts
for your own situation.

> **How to get the most out of this:** run a prompt, then reply to the AI with
> "make it more specific to me: [your details]". The second pass is where the
> gold is. Treat the AI like a sharp consultant who needs context, not a magic
> button.

---

## 💰 PACK 1 — MONEY & INCOME

**1. The 72-Hour Cash Plan**
> You're my no-excuses business coach. I have [skill or asset I can offer], a
> laptop, and Wi-Fi. Build me an exact hour-by-hour plan to earn my first
> $1,000 in 72 hours by selling a service to people who already pay for things.
> 80% of the plan must be outreach and delivery, not planning. Give me the
> outreach message to send and the exact offer + price.

**2. The Skill-to-Offer Translator**
> Here is everything I'm good at: [list skills/hobbies/experience]. Turn each one
> into a specific paid offer with a one-sentence pitch, a target buyer who has
> money, and a starting price. Rank them by fastest-to-first-dollar.

**3. The Cold DM That Gets Replies**
> Write me 5 cold outreach DMs to [type of business] offering [my service]. Each
> must open with a specific observation about *their* business (leave a blank for
> me to fill), promise one clear outcome, and end with a low-friction question.
> No fluff, under 60 words each.

**4. The Price-Raise Script**
> I currently charge [price] for [service]. Write me the exact message to send
> existing clients raising my price to [new price], framed around the results
> I deliver, that keeps them instead of scaring them off.

**5. The Idle-Asset Audit**
> Interview me one question at a time about what I own, know, and have access to.
> After 8 questions, list every way I could turn those underused assets into
> income, ranked by effort-to-payoff.

**6. The Recurring-Revenue Converter**
> I do [one-off service]. Redesign it as a monthly retainer offer: what's
> included, the price, the pitch that makes "ongoing" obviously better than
> "one-time," and the upgrade message to send past one-off clients.

**7. The Objection Crusher**
> List the top 7 reasons a prospect would say no to [my offer], and for each,
> write a calm one-paragraph response that reframes it without being pushy.

**8. The Lazy Upsell**
> A customer just bought [product/service]. Write the single best upsell to offer
> immediately after, why it's a natural next step for them, and the exact wording
> of the offer.

## 📈 PACK 2 — CONTENT & AUDIENCE

**9. The Viral Hook Machine**
> Give me 20 scroll-stopping first-line hooks for a short video about [topic].
> Mix curiosity gaps, bold claims, and "you're doing X wrong" angles. Each under
> 12 words. Rank your top 3 and say why they'll out-perform.

**10. The Faceless Channel Blueprint**
> Design a faceless short-form content channel in the [niche] space: the content
> format, a 30-video content calendar by title, the posting cadence, and exactly
> how it makes money. Make it producible by one person with AI tools.

**11. The One-Idea-to-30-Posts Engine**
> Take this single idea: [your idea]. Spin it into 30 distinct short-video
> concepts — different angles, hooks, and formats — so I never run out of content
> from one core message.

**12. The Comment-Bait Closer**
> For a video about [topic], write 10 caption + call-to-action combos engineered
> to trigger comments and saves (the metrics that push reach), without sounding
> like engagement bait.

**13. The Repurpose Multiplier**
> Here's my long piece of content: [paste]. Cut it into 8 short-form scripts, a
> carousel, an email, and 5 tweets — each able to stand alone and point back to
> the original.

**14. The Niche-Down Finder**
> I want to start in [broad niche] but it's crowded. Give me 10 specific
> sub-niches with less competition and clear buyer intent, and for each, the one
> piece of content I'd post first to test it.

**15. The Story Spine**
> Turn this dry fact or tip — [paste] — into a 30-second video script with a hook,
> a tension/turn, and a payoff. Make me *feel* something, then deliver the value.

**16. The Profile Optimizer**
> Here's my current bio/profile: [paste]. Rewrite it so a stranger instantly
> knows who I help, what I offer, and what to do next. Give me 3 versions.

## 🧠 PACK 3 — THINKING & DECISIONS

**17. The Devil's Advocate**
> I'm about to [decision]. Argue the strongest possible case *against* it. Find
> the holes I'm too excited to see. Then tell me what would have to be true for it
> to actually work.

**18. The Second-Order Thinker**
> I'm considering [action/decision]. Walk me through the consequences: what
> happens next, then what happens *after that*, and after that. Surface the
> non-obvious downstream effects most people miss.

**19. The 10/10/10 Lens**
> I'm torn on [decision]. Help me think it through by asking: how will I feel
> about this in 10 minutes, 10 months, and 10 years? Then give me your read.

**20. The Blind-Spot Interview**
> Ask me 10 sharp questions, one at a time, about [situation/goal]. After the
> last one, tell me the blind spot, false assumption, or risk I clearly haven't
> noticed.

**21. The First-Principles Breakdown**
> Take [problem] and strip it to first principles. What's actually,
> fundamentally true here? Rebuild the best solution from those truths instead of
> from how everyone normally does it.

**22. The Pre-Mortem**
> Imagine it's [timeframe] from now and my [project/goal] failed badly. Write the
> story of exactly how it went wrong, then turn each failure into a safeguard I
> can put in place today.

**23. The Trade-Off Map**
> I'm choosing between [option A] and [option B]. Lay out the real trade-offs side
> by side — not just pros/cons, but what each one *costs* me that I can't get
> back. Then recommend one and own the reasoning.

## ⚡ PACK 4 — PRODUCTIVITY & SYSTEMS

**24. The Priority Knife**
> Here's my to-do list: [paste]. Cut it. Tell me the ONE thing that, if done,
> makes the rest easier or irrelevant. Then order the rest, and name what I should
> delete entirely.

**25. The Anti-Procrastination Unblock**
> I keep avoiding [task]. Ask me why until we hit the real reason, then give me
> the smallest possible first step — something I can do in under 2 minutes — to
> break the freeze.

**26. The System Builder**
> I do [recurring task] over and over. Design a repeatable system or checklist so
> it takes half the time and I stop reinventing it each time. Include a template
> I can reuse.

**27. The Energy Audit**
> Interview me about my typical day. Then map which activities give me energy vs.
> drain it, and redesign my schedule to front-load the high-leverage work into my
> best hours.

**28. The Delegation Drafter**
> Here's a task I do myself: [paste]. Write the exact instructions I'd hand to an
> assistant or AI to do it for me at 90% of my quality, so I can stop doing it.

**29. The Weekly Review Coach**
> Run me through a 10-minute weekly review. Ask me what worked, what didn't, and
> what I'm avoiding — then help me set the 3 priorities for next week.

## 🚀 PACK 5 — LEARNING & GROWTH

**30. The Feynman Tutor**
> Teach me [topic] as if I'm smart but completely new. Use a plain-language
> analogy, then check my understanding with one question before moving on. Don't
> dump it all at once.

**31. The Skill Speedrun**
> I want to get usefully good at [skill] in 30 days with [X hours/week]. Build me
> a week-by-week plan with the exact things to practice, in what order, and how
> I'll know I'm improving.

**32. The Mistake Map**
> I'm learning [skill]. List the 10 most common beginner mistakes, why each one
> happens, and the simple habit that prevents it — so I can skip the painful part.

**33. The Steal-Like-a-Pro Breakdown**
> Here's an example of great [work I admire]: [paste/describe]. Reverse-engineer
> *why* it works — the techniques and choices — so I can apply the same principles
> to my own work.

**34. The Knowledge Gap Finder**
> I want to master [field]. Map the core concepts as a dependency tree (what you
> must understand before what), then tell me where to start and what I can safely
> ignore for now.

## 🗣️ PACK 6 — WRITING & COMMUNICATION

**35. The Make-It-Human Editor**
> Rewrite this so it sounds like a sharp human wrote it, not AI: [paste]. Cut
> filler, vary the sentences, keep my meaning, and lose every cliché. Show me
> what you changed and why.

**36. The Hard Email**
> I need to send a difficult message about [situation] to [person]. Write it so
> I'm honest and firm but not a jerk — clear ask, no groveling, no rambling.
> Give me a short and a slightly warmer version.

**37. The TL;DR + So-What**
> Summarize this: [paste]. Give me a 3-bullet TL;DR, then a one-line "so what" —
> the single thing I should actually *do* about it.

**38. The Tone Shifter**
> Here's my message: [paste]. Give me 3 versions — one warmer, one more
> authoritative, one more casual — so I can pick the right tone for the reader.

**39. The Pitch Tightener**
> Here's how I describe what I do: [paste]. Make it a 10-second pitch a stranger
> instantly gets, a 1-sentence version, and a single tagline. No jargon.

**40. The Negotiation Prep**
> I'm negotiating [situation]. Play the other side and push back hard so I can
> practice. After 4 rounds, coach me on where I gave up too much and the lines
> I should have used.

## ⭐ HOW TO ACTUALLY GET PAID WITH THESE

The prompts are the tool. Here's the loop that turns them into money:

1. **Pick ONE pack** that matches a skill you can already sell.
2. **Use it to deliver a real service** — e.g., use Pack 2 to run someone's
   content, Pack 1 to land the client.
3. **Charge for the outcome, not the prompt.** Nobody pays for "a prompt." They
   pay for the finished ad, the booked calls, the edited videos.
4. **Reinvest** your first wins into doing it again, bigger.

The AI makes you faster. *You* still have to send the offer and do the work —
that's the part no prompt can skip, and it's exactly why most people who buy
something like this never use it. Don't be most people. Run one prompt today.

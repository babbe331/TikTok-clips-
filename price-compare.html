How does this work?
First time: upload your old price list and your new one. We match rows by product name and show every change — +$2.00 if it costs more now, −$2.00 if it's cheaper, plus anything brand-new or dropped.

Then press “💾 Save new list as the baseline.” It's remembered on this device (in your browser). Next time a new list comes in — twice a week or however often — you only add the new one and it auto-loads the saved baseline as the old one, so you never have to dig up the previous list.

Sending the list: use 📋 Copy full list to send to copy a clean text report you can paste straight into an email or text message, or ⬇ Export comparison (CSV) for a spreadsheet.

Add the list any way you have it — CSV, PDF, or a photo:
• 📄 CSV — a spreadsheet with at least a name column and a price column (sell works too); category/unit optional. Most accurate.
• 📕 PDF — drop in a price-list PDF. If it has real text, prices are read directly; if it's a scanned image, it's read by OCR. Either way the result lands in an editable list to fix before comparing.
• 📷 Photo — snap or upload a picture of a printed list and the app reads the prices off it. Works best on clear, well-lit, typed lists.

Photos and scanned PDFs aren't 100% perfect, so every reading drops into an editable list where you can correct any name or price before it compares. Everything stays on your device — nothing is uploaded anywhere.

(03) — THE DESIGNER

Brand story

TABBE Designs was founded in 2024 by Talia Abbe — a New York native with a BS in Textile & Fashion Design from the University of Wisconsin–Madison and an MFA in Fashion Design from the Fashion Institute of Technology.

The brand was born out of the pandemic, when Talia faced her own mental-health struggles in isolation. TABBE exists to end the stigma around mental health — exploring how positive and negative stimuli shape us internally and externally, and turning that tension into clothing.

Prints influenced by cognitive abstraction meet voluminous, inflated silhouettes and vibrant, child-like color — an inclusive, welcoming world where every piece encourages self-expression, emotional release, and freedom.

“Our mission is to challenge mental health stigma while creating clothing that encourages self-expression, emotional release, and freedom.”

# 🌿 Pocketleaf

A houseplant care & watering-reminder app. One file, works offline, no accounts —
everything lives in the browser on your device.

> **Never kill a plant again.** Smart watering reminders, care guides, and a plant
> journal — in your pocket.

This was built as the "real" half of an end-to-end brand demo (brand identity →
marketing creative → working app → deploy). The brand and the launch ad in
`assets/` were generated with Claude + the Canva design MCP.

## Run it
Open **`index.html`** in any browser, or double-click the file. That's it.

## What it does
- **Garden** — every plant sorted by who's thirsty. A 💧 button logs a watering and
  resets the timer.
- **Smart reminders** — each plant has a watering interval; the app shows *Water
  today / overdue / next in N days* and surfaces thirsty plants first.
- **Plant detail** — light + care tips per species, free-form notes, and a watering
  history log.
- **Care guide** — quick care notes for common houseplants (Monstera, snake plant,
  pothos, fiddle-leaf fig, succulents, and more).
- **Backup** — export everything to a `.json` file from the top bar.

First open seeds a few example plants so it isn't empty; your real data replaces them.

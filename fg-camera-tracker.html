Tips for a clean track
  1. Prop the phone up and don't move it — a still camera is the #1 thing for good numbers.
  2. Get the whole flight in frame, with plain background (sky is perfect) behind the arc.
  3. From the side = speed/angle/apex/hang. From behind = left/right + make or miss.
  4. If it follows your leg instead of the ball, open Fine-tune and nudge both sliders up.
This is camera estimation, not radar. A phone can get launch angle, hang time, apex, rough ball speed, and left/right pretty well — but not spin rate, and accuracy depends on a steady camera and an honest scale. Real TrackMan is a $10k+ radar; this is a free, in-browser take in the same spirit.

# Retention SOP — keep churn under 3%/month

Churn is the silent killer of recurring revenue. The financial model shows that
at 3% churn you plateau just under $45k; at 2% you sail past it. **Retention is
the single highest-leverage activity in this business.** Treat it like the
product, because it is.

## The core principle
Clients don't churn because the system stops working — they churn because they
**stop seeing the value**. Your job is to make the value undeniable and monthly.

## The monthly cadence (per client, ~30 min/month)
1. **Send the ROI report on the same day every month.** Leads received,
   % answered in <5 min, appointments booked, deals influenced — translated into
   **dollars** using their average commission. Use the
   [report prompt](../workflows/PROMPTS.md#monthly-report-opus).
2. **Lead with their win.** "Last month the system booked 14 appointments and
   responded to 100% of leads within 60 seconds — that's roughly ${value} in
   pipeline."
3. **Ship one improvement.** Always have shipped *something* — a new source
   integrated, refreshed copy, a new sequence. Visible momentum = retention.
4. **One recommendation for next month.** Keeps you the expert, not the vendor.

## Quarterly
- 20-minute strategy call (Scale tier: included; others: value-add)
- Review pricing — raise on renewal if results justify it
- Ask for a referral and a fresh testimonial

## Early-warning churn signals (act within 48h)
- Stops opening reports → call them, walk through results live
- Results dipped → get ahead of it: explain the cause + the fix before they ask
- Goes quiet for 2+ weeks → personal check-in, not automated
- Mentions budget/cost → reframe ROI in dollars, offer annual prepay discount

## Retention offers that work
- **Annual prepay** at ~2 months free → locks in 12 months, kills churn risk
- **Performance framing** → tie the retainer to deals influenced, not "software"
- **Switching cost** → the longer they run, the more historical data/optimization
  they'd lose by leaving. Make that real and visible in the dashboard.

# Client Intake Form (build in Tally/Typeform)

Send at signing. Captures everything you need to build without a dozen
back-and-forth emails. Keep it to one sitting (~10 min).

## Section 1 — The basics
1. Team/brokerage name
2. Primary contact (name, email, mobile)
3. Agents who will receive booked appointments (name, email, calendar link)
4. Markets/areas you serve
5. Your average commission per closed deal (used for ROI reporting)

## Section 2 — Lead sources
6. Where do leads come from today? (Zillow, Realtor.com, Facebook/IG ads,
   website form, open houses, referrals — check all)
7. Approx. leads/month
8. Current average response time, honestly?
9. What CRM do you use? (Follow Up Boss / HubSpot / kvCore / GoHighLevel / other)

## Section 3 — Voice & brand
10. Paste 2–3 of your best past texts/emails to leads (for voice matching)
11. Tone you want (warm & casual / polished & professional / high-energy)
12. Anything you never want said? (banned phrases, over-promises)
13. Booking link(s) and your scheduling rules (hours, buffer, round-robin?)

## Section 4 — Tech access (collected securely, not in this form)
14. Sending domain/email for outbound
15. Who can grant CRM + lead-source access, and when?

## Section 5 — Goals & guardrails
16. What does success look like in 90 days? (e.g. "respond to every lead in
    under 5 min and book 15 extra appointments/month")
17. Quiet hours / compliance preferences for SMS
18. Anything else I should know?

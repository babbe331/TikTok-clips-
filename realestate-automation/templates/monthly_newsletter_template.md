# Monthly Market Newsletter — the long-nurture endpoint

This is where leads land at the end of the 90-day sequence (touch #14) instead
of dying. A once-a-month, genuinely useful email keeps the client's brand in
front of every past lead — so the person who buys 8 months from now still buys
through them. You set this up once per client as a recurring send.

> Default market: Austin, TX.

---

**Subject lines that get opened (rotate):**
- "{Month} in {City}: what your neighbors' homes sold for"
- "3 things happening in the {City} market right now"
- "Thinking of moving in {year}? Read this first"

---

**{City} Market Update — {Month Year}**
From {Agent Name}, {Brokerage}

Hi {First},

Quick monthly snapshot of what's happening in {City} real estate:

**📈 The numbers**
- Median sale price: {$X} ({up/down Y%} vs last month)
- Average days on market: {Z}
- Inventory: {tight / loosening}

**🏡 What it means for you**
{2–3 sentences of plain-English interpretation — are buyers or sellers favored,
is it a good time to act, what to watch. This is the value; make it real.}

**🔑 Featured listings**
{2–3 fresh listings with photos + a one-line hook each.}

**💬 From {Agent Name}**
{One short, human paragraph — a tip, a story, a heads-up about a neighborhood.}

Whenever you're ready to make a move — this year or next — just reply. No
pressure, I'm here when the timing's right.

{Agent Name} · {phone} · {booking link}

*You're receiving this because you inquired about {City} homes. Unsubscribe
anytime — {unsubscribe link}.*

---

*Build notes: schedule as a monthly recurring send in the client's email tool.
Pull the numbers from the local MLS/market reports. The interpretation + the
human paragraph are what keep people subscribed — automate the layout, not the
voice.*

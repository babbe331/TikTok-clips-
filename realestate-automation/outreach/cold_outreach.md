# Outreach Scripts — landing your first 10–15 clients

Direct outreach is your engine until SEO and referrals take over (~month 6).
**Target:** team leads / brokers / owners of 3–15 agent residential teams.
**Volume goal:** 30 personalized touches/day → ~10 booked demos/month →
2–4 closes/month. Personalization beats volume; do fewer, better.

**Find them:** local brokerage sites, Zillow team pages, "top real estate teams
[city]," LinkedIn (title: Team Lead / Broker / Owner + Real Estate), Instagram
(agents are very active there).

---

## The angle that works

Don't sell "AI" or "automation." Sell **the leak**: they pay for leads and lose
most of them. Lead with a specific, true observation about *their* business.

---

## Cold Email — v1 (the leak)

> **Subject:** quick question about your Zillow leads
>
> Hi {FirstName},
>
> Saw {Team Name} is active on {Zillow/your site} — you're clearly putting real
> money into leads.
>
> Quick question: when a new lead comes in at 9pm, how fast does someone reply?
>
> Most teams I see take a few hours, and ~50% of those leads quietly go cold.
> I build a system that texts every new lead within 60 seconds, qualifies them,
> and books the hot ones straight onto an agent's calendar — then follows up
> 14 times so nothing slips.
>
> Worth a 12-minute look? I'll show you exactly how it'd work on your lead flow.
>
> {Your Name}
> P.S. I made a 3-min video of the system in action — want the link?

## Cold Email — v2 (the follow-up, 3 days later)

> **Subject:** re: quick question about your Zillow leads
>
> Hi {FirstName}, floating this back up. One extra closed deal a month from
> faster follow-up would more than cover the whole system. Here's the 3-min
> demo either way: {Loom link}. Want me to run a free audit of your current
> lead response time? — {Your Name}

## Instagram / LinkedIn DM

> Hey {FirstName} — love what {Team Name} is doing in {city}. Quick one: do your
> web/Zillow leads get an instant text back, or does it depend on who's free?
> I set up an AI that replies in 60s + follows up for 90 days so teams stop
> losing leads they already paid for. Happy to send a 3-min demo if useful — no
> pitch.

*(Wait for a reply before sending anything else. The question does the work.)*

## Voicemail / cold call opener

> under a minute and following up automatically. I made a short video showing

## The Loom demo script (record ONCE, reuse forever)

Keep it under 4 minutes. Screen-record the actual workflow.

1. **(0:00–0:20) The hook.** "If you're buying leads and replying in hours, this
   3 minutes will probably pay for itself. Here's what happens to a new lead the
   second it comes in."
2. **(0:20–1:30) Lead Qualifier live.** Submit a fake lead on screen → show the
   60-second AI text back → show it scoring and booking a hot lead.
3. **(1:30–2:30) Follow-Up Engine.** Show the 14-touch sequence and explain
   "this runs for 90 days whether your agents remember or not."
4. **(2:30–3:15) Onboarding.** Show the won-deal automation firing the welcome
   packet + agreement.
5. **(3:15–3:45) The ROI + CTA.** "This costs less than one commission and is
   built to find you at least one extra deal a month. If you want, I'll set the
   whole thing up on your lead flow — book a 15-min call here: {link}."

## Daily outreach checklist

- [ ] 15 personalized cold emails (real observation in line 1)
- [ ] 10 DMs (IG/LinkedIn) — question-first, no pitch
- [ ] 5 follow-ups to people who opened but didn't reply
- [ ] Log every touch + reply in your own CRM (you eat your own dog food)
- [ ] Book any positive reply into a 15-min call same week

## Handling the 3 objections

| They say | You say |
| --- | --- |
| "We already follow up." | "Most teams think so — can I run a free test? I'll submit a lead to your site and we'll time the response together. If you're already fast, you've lost nothing." |
| "Too expensive." | "It's less than one commission. If it finds you one extra deal a quarter it's paid for 3x over. Want me to model it on your numbers?" |
| "We don't have time to set it up." | "That's the point — I do all of it. You give me access once and it runs. Onboarding is ~2 weeks and you do almost nothing." |

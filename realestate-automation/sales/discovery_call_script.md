# Discovery / Sales Call Script — the 15-minute close

You'll be on these calls. This is the agenda to run every time. Goal: **diagnose
their leak, quantify it in dollars, and book the build** — not to "convince."
Talk 30%, listen 70%. The numbers do the selling.

> Default market in examples: Austin, TX. Swap for your city.

---

## Before the call (2 min)
- Glance at their website/Zillow page. Note their lead sources and team size.
- Have the [ROI math](../seo/articles/roi-of-speed-to-lead.md) ready.
- Open the [audit report template](audit_report_template.md) to fill live.

## 1. Frame (30 sec)
> "Thanks for hopping on. I'll keep this to 15 minutes. I want to understand how
> leads flow through your team right now, show you where I usually see teams
> lose them, and if it's a fit, walk you through what I'd build. Sound good?"

## 2. Diagnose — ask, then shut up (5 min)
- "Where do most of your leads come from today?" *(Zillow, FB, site…)*
- "Roughly how many a month?"
- "Walk me through what happens the second a new lead comes in at, say, 8pm."
- "Who follows up, and how many times before you let one go?"
- "What's your rough conversion from lead to closed deal?"
- "What's an average commission for your team?"

*Write every number into the audit report as they talk. These are the inputs to
the close.*

## 3. Reflect the leak back (2 min)
> "So you're paying for ~{N} leads a month, the first response can take a few
> hours, and follow-up usually stops after a couple touches. Here's what that's
> costing you…"

Do the math out loud:
> "At {conv}% you're closing ~{deals} a month. If we tighten response time and
> run a full 90-day follow-up, a one-point lift is realistic — that's **one more
> deal a month, about ${commission}.** Every month. From leads you already pay
> for."

*Let that land. Silence is fine.*

## 4. Show the fix (4 min)
Share screen → play the 4-min Loom OR demo the live workflow:
- Lead comes in → 60-second text-back
- AI qualifies + books the hot one
- 90-day follow-up runs itself
- Won deal → onboarding fires

> "That's it. You give me access once, I build it on your CRM in about two
> weeks, and it runs."

## 5. Price + close (2 min)
> "For a team your size I'd recommend the **Growth** package — all three systems,
> email and SMS. It's $2,500 to build and $3,000 a month. That's less than a
> third of one commission, and it's designed to find you at least one extra deal
> a month. And if it's not live and responding within 14 days, that month's free."

Then ask for the decision directly:
> "Want me to send the agreement and get you onboarded this week?"

## 6. Handle the 3 objections
| They say | You say |
| --- | --- |
| "Let me think about it." | "Totally fair. What's the one thing you'd need to be sure of? Let's settle it now." |
| "It's expensive." | "Compared to what — one lost deal is 3x this. Want me to model it on your exact numbers before you decide?" |
| "We're too busy to set it up." | "That's the whole point — I do all of it. You grant access once and it runs. Onboarding is ~2 weeks and you do almost nothing." |

## 7. Next step (always book something)
- Closed → send agreement + onboarding link same day.
- Not yet → "I'll send you the free audit report from today's numbers and a
  calendar link. When's good to reconnect — Thursday?"

**Never end a call without a scheduled next action.**

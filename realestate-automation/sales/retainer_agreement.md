# Service Retainer Agreement — TEMPLATE

> ⚠️ **Not legal advice.** This is a plain-language starting point. Have a
> lawyer in your jurisdiction review before you use it. Brackets are fill-ins.

**This Agreement** is between **{Your Agency LLC}** ("Provider") and
**{Client Brokerage}** ("Client"), effective **{date}**.

### 1. Services
Provider will build, deploy, host, monitor, and optimize the "RealtyFlow System"
— automated lead qualification, multi-channel follow-up, and client onboarding
workflows — as described in the accompanying Proposal ({tier}).

### 2. Fees
- **Setup fee:** ${setup}, due on signing. Non-refundable once build begins.
- **Monthly retainer:** ${retainer}/month, billed in advance on the {1st} of
  each month via {Stripe/ACH}.
- **Pass-through costs** (SMS/Twilio, email, AI usage) are {included up to
  {cap} / billed at cost + {0%}}.

### 3. Term & Termination
- Initial term: **90 days** from go-live, then **month-to-month**.
- Either party may cancel with **30 days' written notice** after the initial
  term. No refunds of prepaid months; service runs through the paid period.

### 4. Client Responsibilities
Client will provide timely access to its CRM, lead sources, sending domain, and
branding, and is responsible for the accuracy and lawful sourcing of its leads
and for obtaining all required consents from leads (see §6).

### 5. Performance & Guarantee
Provider will have the system live and responding to leads within **14 days** of
receiving all required access; if not, Client's first monthly retainer is
waived. Provider does **not** guarantee specific sales results, which depend on
factors outside Provider's control (lead quality, agent activity, market).

### 6. Compliance
Client and Provider will operate the system in compliance with applicable law,
including TCPA/SMS consent and opt-out requirements and Fair Housing law.
Provider will implement STOP/opt-out handling and will not configure targeting
based on protected classes. Client warrants its leads have provided the consents
necessary to be contacted.

### 7. Data & Confidentiality
Each party keeps the other's confidential information confidential. Client owns
its lead/customer data; Provider may use aggregated, anonymized performance data
to improve its services. On termination, Provider will export Client's
workflows/data and cease processing within {15} days.

### 8. Ownership
Provider retains ownership of its reusable templates, prompts, and workflow
frameworks. Client receives a license to use the deployed system for the term.
Client owns its own content, branding, and data.

### 9. Liability
Provider's total liability is limited to the fees paid in the **prior 3 months**.
Neither party is liable for indirect or consequential damages.

### 10. Independent Contractor
Provider is an independent contractor, not an employee or agent of Client.

# Keyword Map — the compounding traffic asset

SEO is the flywheel that replaces cold outreach by ~month 6. Every article is a
salesperson that works forever at near-zero marginal cost. Target the exact
phrases an agency owner types when they feel the pain.

## Buyer intent: who's searching and why

Your buyer is an agency owner/team lead, not a consumer. Write for *them*, not
for homebuyers (that's a different, lower-value audience).

## On-page rules (every article)
- One primary keyword in: title, H1, URL slug, first 100 words, one H2.
- 1,200–2,000 words, genuinely useful, original data/examples where possible.
- **Every article ends with the same CTA:** a free "lead response time audit" or
  the 3-min demo Loom → booking link. This is how traffic becomes pipeline.
- Internal-link the clusters together (topical authority).
- Add an FAQ block targeting the "how/what/how many" long-tails.

## Distribution (don't just publish and pray)
- Repurpose each article into a LinkedIn post + an IG carousel + an email to your
  list. One article = 4+ assets.
- Answer the same questions in real estate Facebook groups / Reddit, linking
  where allowed.
- Build backlinks via guest posts on real estate coaching / proptech blogs.

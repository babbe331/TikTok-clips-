# Zillow Lead Automation: How to Respond in 60 Seconds (Step by Step)

Zillow leads are some of the most expensive in real estate — and some of the
most wasted. They're high-intent (someone's actively looking at homes), but they
also shop multiple agents at once, so they go cold within minutes. If you're not
first, you're often nowhere.

Automation fixes this. Here's how to build a Zillow lead automation that
responds instantly, qualifies automatically, and follows up for months.

## Why Zillow leads need automation more than any other source

- **They're shopping in real time.** A Zillow lead is comparing agents the
  moment they inquire. Speed isn't a nice-to-have; it's the whole ballgame.
- **They're costly.** Every lead you let go cold is money you already spent.
- **They come at all hours.** Nights and weekends are peak browsing time —
  exactly when your team is least available.

## The four pieces of a Zillow automation

### 1. Capture the lead instantly
Zillow delivers leads through its Premier Agent flow and can pass them to your
CRM. The key is getting that lead into an automation **the second it arrives** —
via your CRM's webhook or a connector like Zapier/Make into a workflow tool.

### 2. Instant AI text-back (under 60 seconds)
The moment the lead lands, fire a warm, personal text: *"Hi Sarah, it's Jordan
with Smith Group — saw you're interested in the Maple St area. Are you hoping to
buy in the next few months or just starting to look?"* This single step beats
most of your competition, who are still asleep or busy.

### 3. AI qualification and routing
When the lead replies, an AI assistant reads the conversation, scores intent,
and captures budget, timeline, and financing. Genuinely ready buyers get a
calendar link and book directly onto an agent's schedule. Everyone else drops
into nurture — no lead is lost.

### 4. 90-day follow-up
Most Zillow leads aren't ready today. An automated 14-touch sequence across SMS
and email keeps you top-of-mind until they are — running whether or not your
agents remember.

## Build it yourself or have it done

- **DIY:** Connect Zillow → your CRM → a tool like n8n or Make → an SMS provider
  and an AI model. Budget real time for deliverability, compliance (TCPA consent,
  STOP handling), and copywriting.
- **Done-for-you:** Have the whole pipeline built, integrated with your CRM, and
  monitored on a retainer so it stays optimized.

Either way: the goal is that **no Zillow lead you paid for ever waits more than
60 seconds for a response.**

## A quick compliance note

Only text leads who've consented, honor opt-outs instantly, and never target or
infer protected classes. Good automation has this built in — it protects both
your results (deliverability) and your license.

# Why Real Estate Teams Lose Half Their Leads (And the 60-Second Fix)

If you're a team lead buying leads from Zillow, Facebook, or Google, here's an
uncomfortable truth: **you're probably losing 40–60% of them before an agent
ever has a real conversation.** Not because the leads are bad. Because nobody
got back to them fast enough.

This is the speed-to-lead problem, and it's the single most fixable leak in a
real estate business.

## What "speed to lead" actually means

Speed to lead is the time between a lead raising their hand — submitting a form,
replying to an ad, requesting a showing — and someone on your team making
meaningful contact. The research across industries is brutally consistent:

- The odds of qualifying a lead drop sharply after the **first 5 minutes**.
- Responding in minutes instead of hours can multiply your contact rate several
  times over.
- The first team to respond very often wins the deal — buyers reach out to
  multiple agents and go with whoever shows up first.

A real estate lead is most motivated **the moment they hit submit.** An hour
later they're at dinner. A day later they've talked to two other agents.

## Why teams are slow (it's not laziness)

- Leads arrive at 9pm, on weekends, during showings — exactly when agents are
  busy or off.
- Whoever's "on" has to notice, read, and write a thoughtful reply.
- After the first day, follow-up gets inconsistent and then stops.

You can't out-hustle this with willpower. A human team cannot reliably respond
to every lead in under five minutes, 24/7, forever. **A system can.**

## The 60-second fix

The fix is an automated first response that fires the instant a lead comes in:

1. **Instant text-back.** Within ~60 seconds, the lead gets a warm, personal
   text referencing what they asked about. ("Hi Sarah, saw you're interested in
   the Maple St area — are you hoping to buy in the next few months?")
2. **Qualify automatically.** An AI assistant reads the reply and the lead's
   info, scores intent, and captures budget, timeline, and financing.
3. **Book the hot ones immediately.** Genuinely ready buyers get a calendar link
   and land straight on an agent's schedule — while they're still interested.
4. **Nurture the rest.** Everyone else enters a 90-day follow-up sequence so no
   lead is ever dropped.

None of this replaces your agents. It makes sure your agents only spend time on
leads that are awake, qualified, and ready to talk.

## What it's worth

Run your own numbers. If you buy 100 leads a month and improve your conversion
by even one percentage point, that's one extra deal — often **$8,000–$12,000 in
commission.** The system that does this costs a fraction of a single
commission. The ROI math is not close.

## How to know if you have this problem

Try this today: have a friend submit a lead on your website at 8pm. Time how
long until someone responds. If it's more than five minutes — and for most teams
it's hours — you've just watched money walk out the door in real time.

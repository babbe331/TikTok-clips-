# How Many Times Should You Follow Up With a Real Estate Lead?

Short answer: **more than you think, and far more than you currently do.** The
gap between how many times deals actually require and how many times the average
agent follows up is the single biggest reason leads go to waste.

## The numbers behind follow-up

Across sales — including real estate — the pattern is remarkably stable:

- A large share of deals close somewhere between the **5th and 12th** contact.
- Yet a majority of agents make **two or fewer** follow-up attempts before
  giving up on a lead.
- That means most agents quit *before the point where most deals are won.*

Think about what that implies. You're not losing leads because they weren't
interested. You're losing them because you stopped showing up right before they
were ready.

## Why "more" isn't the whole answer

Volume alone isn't the goal — relevance and timing are. Twelve identical "just
checking in" texts will get you blocked, not booked. The right model is:

1. **Front-load the early days.** Most replies come in the first week. Touch
   quickly: minutes, then hours, then days.
2. **Mix channels.** Alternate SMS (immediate) and email (substance — listings,
   market updates, guides).
3. **Add value, not pressure.** Each touch should give them something: a
   listing, a market insight, a helpful answer.
4. **Taper, don't vanish.** Stretch the cadence out to 30, 60, 90 days so you
   stay top-of-mind without becoming noise.
5. **Have a graceful exit.** A final "I'll pause for now — reach out anytime"
   message reliably reactivates leads who'd gone quiet.

## A proven 90-day cadence

A simple, effective backbone is **14 touches over 90 days**: 0 minutes, 1 hour,
day 1, day 3, day 7, day 10, day 14, day 21, day 30, day 45, day 60, day 75,
and two on day 90 (a "breakup" text and a newsletter invite). Roughly half SMS,
half email.

## The catch: humans can't sustain it

Here's why knowing the right number doesn't fix the problem on its own. No agent
juggling showings and closings can reliably deliver 14 personalized touches to
hundreds of leads at different stages, on time, forever. The intention is there;
the bandwidth isn't.

This is exactly what automation solves. A follow-up system runs the full cadence
on every lead — personalized in the agent's voice — and stops the instant a lead
replies, books, or opts out. Your agents step in only when there's a real
conversation to have.

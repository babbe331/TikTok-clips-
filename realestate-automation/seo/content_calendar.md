# Content Calendar — 2 articles/week, 90-day kickstart

Consistency beats intensity. Two genuinely useful posts a week for a year is 100+
ranked assets. Batch-write with AI assistance, but **always add real expertise,
your own client data, and a human edit** — generic AI content doesn't rank or
convert.

## The repeatable weekly rhythm
- **Mon:** publish Article A (pillar/how-to). Repurpose → LinkedIn + email.
- **Thu:** publish Article B (data/FAQ/comparison). Repurpose → IG carousel.
- **Daily:** 1 social post drawn from the week's articles.

## KPIs to watch (monthly)
- Articles published (target: 8/mo)
- Organic sessions + keywords ranking top-20
- **Audit/demo requests from organic** ← the only metric that pays
- Assisted conversions: leads who read ≥1 article before booking

## When to shift from outreach to SEO
Keep cold outreach as your #1 channel until organic produces **≥4 qualified
demo requests/month** (usually months 5–7). Then taper outreach and reinvest the
time into more content + client delivery.

# Brand Guide — RealtyFlow

Consistency makes a one-person business look like a real company. Use these
everywhere (site, decks, reports, emails). All swappable if you rename.

## Name
**RealtyFlow** — working brand. Before committing: check the domain is free and
do a quick trademark/Google search to avoid collisions, then lock the `.com`.

## Logo
`realtyflow_logo.png` (in this folder) — generated wordmark + house/flow icon.
Use on white or very light backgrounds. Keep clear space around it.

## Colors
| Role | Hex | Use |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Ink (navy) | `#0f172a` | Headlines, body text |
| Brand blue | `#1d4ed8` | Buttons, links, "Flow", accents |
| Brand dark | `#1e40af` | Button hover |
| Accent sky | `#0ea5e9` | Gradients, highlights |
| Soft | `#f1f5f9` | Section backgrounds |
| Good (green) | `#16a34a` | Checks, positive metrics |
| Muted | `#64748b` | Secondary text |

## Type
System sans-serif stack (`-apple-system, "Segoe UI", Roboto, Arial`). Free,
fast, looks native everywhere. Headlines bold + tight letter-spacing; body 1.6
line-height. (Already wired into `index.html` and `dashboard.html`.)

## Voice
- **Direct, confident, numbers-first.** Lead with the leak and the dollars.
- **Plain English, no jargon.** Your buyers are agents, not engineers.
- **Honest.** Never promise specific sales results; promise speed, consistency,
  and ROI math. Honesty is a sales advantage in this market.

## Tagline options
- "Stop losing the leads you already paid for."  ← default, used on site
- "Every lead answered in 60 seconds."
- "Your follow-up, on autopilot."

Physics model: numerical ballistic integration with quadratic aerodynamic drag and Magnus lift on a regulation football (0.41 kg). Goalposts: NFL spec — 18.5 ft wide, 10 ft crossbar. Figures are simulated estimates, not radar measurements. Built as a TrackMan-style visualizer for field goals.

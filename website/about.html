Our story

Fashion for the mind

TABBE Designs turns feeling into form. Founded by Talia Abbe, the brand explores how the world's positive and negative stimuli affect us — inside and out — and challenges the stigma around mental health through clothing you can feel something in.

The designer

Talia Abbe

A New York native, Talia earned a BS in Textile & Fashion Design with a certificate in Entrepreneurship from the University of Wisconsin–Madison, then an MFA in Fashion Design from the Fashion Institute of Technology (FIT).

Originating as a creative response to the emotional toll of isolation in 2020, her work uses inflated latex to create exaggerated, uplifting shapes — amplified by a vibrant palette. The clothes evoke child-like playfulness, underpinned by an adult sensibility.

"Our mission is to challenge mental health stigma while creating clothing that encourages self-expression, emotional release, and freedom."

How do I update my inventory from a spreadsheet?
Save each spreadsheet as a .csv file (in Excel or Google Sheets: File → Download/Save As → CSV), then click Update inventory. You can pick several files at once — hold Ctrl/⌘ while selecting. We'll look for columns named name, quantity (or qty/on hand), unit, price, cost, category, and low stock. Column order and capitalization don't matter.

After reading the files you'll choose what to do: Replace wipes the current inventory and loads only what's in your spreadsheets, or Add & update keeps what's there and updates matching items by name.

Pricing

Your costs and sell prices. Import a price list to update them.

🏷️ Your three customer prices — markup above cost

Each customer is set to one of these tiers. The prices in the table are your cost plus the markup. Change a % here and every price (and every customer on that tier) updates instantly.

How do I import a price list?
Save your price sheet as a .csv file, then click Import price list (you can pick several files at once). We match rows to your products by name and update their price (sell price) and cost. You'll choose whether to only update products you already have, or to also add any new products found in the price list.

Recognized columns (any order, capitalization doesn't matter): name, price (or sell), cost, and optionally category / unit.

Earnings

What each sale brings in — before your margin (the sale total) and after (your profit).

Profit is the sale total minus your cost per unit (from the Pricing tab). Set a cost on each product to see accurate profit.

Customers

The companies you sell to, and the pricing tier each one gets.

How do I import customers (with their margins)?
Save your customer sheet as a .csv and click Import customers. Recognized columns: company/name, contact, phone, email, notes, and either a tier column (high/medium/low) or a markup/margin number (e.g. 40) for a custom % above cost. Matching names are updated; new ones are added.

📥 Add order from a Slack message

Paste the message exactly as it comes in on Slack. First line is the customer, then one line per item — like three heirloom tomatoes.

🔗 Connect Slack

Once the connector is set up (see slack-connector/README), paste its web address and key here. After that, orders posted in your Slack channel show up automatically — no extra steps.

How does this work?
A tiny always-on “connector” listens to your Slack channel. When someone posts an order, it parses it and this app pulls it in automatically every 30 seconds while open (and catches up on anything posted while it was closed). Setup steps are in the slack-connector folder of your project.

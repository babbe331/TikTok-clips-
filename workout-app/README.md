# Daily Fit 💪 — Daily Workout & Knee Rehab App

A simple, private workout app you sign into every day. It logs the workout you
did and gives you **three knee-rehab exercises every day**, plus a full daily
workout plan tailored for a **56-year-old woman who wants to lose weight, get
toned, and into great shape** — all low-impact and joint-friendly.

There's a **language button in the top-right corner** to switch between
**English 🇬🇧 and Portuguese 🇧🇷** instantly.

## How to use it

It's a single file — no installation needed.

1. Open `workout-app/index.html` in any web browser (double-click it, or drag
   it into Chrome/Safari/Edge). It works great on a phone too.
2. **Sign in:** the first time, type your name and pick a 4-digit PIN. After
   that, you sign in with the same name + PIN every day.
3. Each day you'll see:
   - 🔥 **Today's workout** — rotates through legs/glutes, arms/core,
     a fat-burn circuit, and an active-recovery day.
   - 🦵 **3 knee rehab exercises** — gentle strengthening moves that change
     every day.
   - 📝 **Log your workout** — write what you did and tap how it felt.
4. Your **streak, total workouts, and weekly count** show at the top to keep
   you motivated. All history is saved.

## Putting it on your phone

- Email yourself the `index.html` file and open it, **or**
- Open it in your phone browser and use **"Add to Home Screen"** so it feels
  like a real app.

## Where is my data?

Everything is stored **locally in your browser** (`localStorage`) under your
name. Nothing is uploaded anywhere — it's completely private. Use the same
browser/device to keep your history.

## Switch language

Tap the **🇧🇷 PT / 🇬🇧 EN** button in the top-right corner any time. The whole
app — including the workouts and exercises — switches instantly.

# Daily Fit 💪 — Sueli's Daily Workout & Knee Rehab App

A simple, private, **illustrated** workout app you sign into every day. It greets
**Sueli** by name, logs the workout you did, and gives you **three knee-rehab
exercises every day** plus a full daily workout plan tailored for a
**56-year-old woman who wants to lose weight, get toned, and into great shape** —
all low-impact and joint-friendly.

There's a **language button in the top-right corner** to switch between
**English 🇬🇧 and Portuguese 🇧🇷** instantly, and a **🔔 reminder** that nudges her
at **7:00 PM** on days she hasn't logged a workout.

## What's inside

- 👋 **"Welcome, Sueli!"** banner every time the app opens.
- 📸 **A real photo for every exercise** — actual demonstration photos (from the
  open-source free-exercise-db) downloaded into the app, so they always load and
  work offline.
- 🔥 **Full ~45-minute workouts** — each day has a warm-up, main strength/toning
  block, and a cool-down stretch, with a time estimate per exercise and a total.
  6 rotating day-types: legs & glutes, arms & core, fat-burn circuit, core &
  posture, full-body strength, and active recovery.
- 🗓️ **Choose your workout days** — pick how many days per week (e.g. 4) and
  which ones. Reminders only fire on those days; other days show a "rest day"
  card (knee exercises still appear every day).
- 🦵 **3 fresh knee-rehab exercises every day**, with photos, sets/reps and tips.
- 📝 **Log your workout** and how it felt (😮‍💨🙂😀🤩).
- 📈 **Progress tab** — day streak, total workouts, weekly count, full history.
- 💡 **Tips tab** — friendly habits for weight loss and toning after 50.
- 🔔 **7 PM reminder** notifications (see note below).
- 📱 Easy **bottom-tab navigation**: Today · Log · Progress · Tips.

## How to use it

It's a single app — no installation, no accounts, no servers.

1. Open `workout-app/index.html` in any browser (works great on a phone).
2. **Sign in:** the name is already set to **Sueli** — just pick a 4-digit PIN
   the first time. After that, enter the same PIN to sign in each day.
3. Use the bottom tabs to move around. Tap **Log** to record your workout.

## 📱 Put it on your phone (recommended)

For the best experience — and so the **7 PM reminder** can reach you — add it to
your home screen:

- **iPhone (Safari):** open the page → Share → **Add to Home Screen**.
- **Android (Chrome):** open the page → menu (⋮) → **Add to Home screen / Install app**.

Then open it from the new icon like a normal app.

## 🔔 About the 7 PM reminder

Tap **Log → "Turn on 7 PM reminder"** and allow notifications. On any day you
haven't logged a workout, you'll get a friendly nudge at **7:00 PM**.

**Important — how reminders work here:** because this is a private app with **no
server**, reminders are scheduled by the app itself. They fire reliably when the
app is **installed to your home screen / open in the background**, and you'll
also get an instant reminder if you open the app after 7 PM without logging. A
guaranteed push when the phone is fully closed for hours would need an
always-on server — tell me if you'd like that and I can add it.

> 💡 To get notifications working at all, the app must be opened from a web
> address (https) or installed to the home screen — opening the raw file
> directly (`file://`) won't allow notifications. Easiest path: host it for free
> (e.g. GitHub Pages) and "Add to Home Screen." I can set that up for you.

## Switch language

Tap the **🇧🇷 PT / 🇬🇧 EN** button in the top-right corner any time — the whole
app, including every workout and exercise, switches instantly.

## Where is my data?

Everything is stored **locally in the browser** under Sueli's name. Nothing is
uploaded anywhere — it's completely private. Use the same browser/device to keep
your history.
